Concrete Material Estimator.

Estimate approximate cement, sand and coarse aggregate quantities from concrete volume and a nominal mix ratio.

Estimate materials

Enter a concrete volume to estimate materials.
Approximate educational estimate using a 1.54 dry-volume factor and 0.0347 m³ per 50 kg cement bag. Verify specifications before procurement or construction.

How it works

The calculator converts the wet concrete volume to an approximate dry material volume, then divides that volume according to the selected nominal mix.

  • 1 : 1.5 : 3
  • 1 : 2 : 4
  • 1 : 3 : 6
