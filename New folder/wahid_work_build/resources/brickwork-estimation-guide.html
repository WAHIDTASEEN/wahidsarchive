CONSTRUCTION ESTIMATION GUIDE

Brickwork Estimation: Wall Volume, Bricks and Mortar

A simple method for preliminary brickwork quantity estimation using wall dimensions, brick size and mortar assumptions.

Start with wall volume

The first step is to calculate the gross wall volume from length, height and thickness.

Wall volume = Length × Height × Thickness

Openings such as doors and windows should be deducted when the problem or measurement method requires a net wall quantity.

Estimating brick quantity

Brick count depends on the actual brick dimensions and the assumed mortar joint. A useful method is to calculate the nominal volume occupied by one brick including its mortar allowance, then divide the wall volume by that nominal unit volume.

Approximate brick count = Wall volume ÷ nominal brick-with-mortar volume

Mortar

Mortar quantity is not simply the wall volume multiplied by an arbitrary percentage. A more transparent approach is to calculate the total volume of brick units plus the volume occupied by joints, then apply the selected mortar mix and dry-volume allowance.

Checklist

  • Confirm brick dimensions.
  • Confirm wall thickness.
  • Deduct openings when required.
  • State the mortar-joint assumption.
  • State the wastage allowance.
  • Keep wet and dry volumes clearly separated.
Brickwork estimates are sensitive to brick size and joint thickness. Always show the assumptions so another person can reproduce the quantity.
