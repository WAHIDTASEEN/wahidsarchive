Estimate wall volume, approximate brick count and basic mortar material quantities for preliminary academic calculations.

Estimate brickwork

Enter wall dimensions.
Illustrative estimator. Brick size, joint thickness, openings, wastage and local specifications can materially change quantities.

Assumptions

This first version uses approximately 500 bricks per cubic metre and a 1:6 cement:sand mortar assumption for an educational estimate.

  • Wall volume = L × H × T
  • Approx. bricks = wall volume × 500
  • Approx. wet mortar = wall volume × 0.25
  • Dry mortar factor = 1.33
